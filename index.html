✓ Winter Maintenance Decision Rules




Route-by-route decision logic







Priority
Ground temperature threshold

Plan section 3:
If the route does not reach its temperature threshold,
do not treat.





Wetness:
Rain or a dew point within 1°C of the ground
indicates potential moisture.
A dry road below zero does not automatically
require treatment under this exercise.





Plan section 5:
Priority 3 routes require a depot stock level
of at least 40%. Priority 1 routes take precedence
when salt is limited.





Resources:
Check the available salt, trucks and drivers.
Flag insufficient resources to the engineer.





Plan section 4:
Finish spreading one hour before the ground
reaches 0°C. Allow one hour per 20 km.
Never leave before 17:00.
